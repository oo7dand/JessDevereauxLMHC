About Jess

Jess Devereaux, LMHC, is a licensed mental health counselor who provides calm, collaborative therapy for children, teens, adults, and families. Her style is warm, down-to-earth, and focused on building a safe space where you can be honest without feeling judged.

Jess integrates mindfulness, creative expression, and evidence-based approaches — always centering each client’s goals, values, and pace. Her mission is to create a supportive, authentic space where young people and their families feel truly seen and understood, while building confidence, coping skills, and resilience.

Trauma-informed • LGBTQ+ affirming • Culturally aware

Services

Child & Adolescent Therapy

Supporting children and teens in feeling seen, heard, and validated as they navigate life’s challenges. Sessions help young people express themselves, build confidence, and develop emotional clarity.

Creative & Mindful Counseling for Youth

A gentle, expressive space where young people can explore emotions, process stress, and discover strengths through mindfulness, creative tools, and age-appropriate therapeutic techniques.

Emotional Growth & Resilience Building

Guiding children and teens toward emotional balance, coping skills, and self-understanding. We focus on nurturing resilience so they can move through difficult experiences with greater calm and confidence.

Family Support & Parent Collaboration

Helping families understand emotional needs, strengthen communication, and better support their child or teen. Collaboration empowers parents and caregivers to create a more connected, grounded home environment.

Confidence, Connection & Self-Awareness Coaching

Supporting youth in building a strong sense of self, navigating friendships, increasing confidence, and developing tools to handle anxiety, stress, and big emotions.

Mind-Body Integration for Kids & Teens

Therapy that integrates mind, body, and emotional awareness to help children and adolescents feel grounded, centered, and more connected to themselves.

Coping Skills & Emotional Regulation

Helping young people understand their emotions, develop healthy coping strategies, and build the resilience needed to manage stress, overwhelm, and everyday challenges.

Strength-Based Support for Growing Minds

Mindful, creative, and relational support for kids and teens as they explore identity, find their voice, and discover their inner strength.
