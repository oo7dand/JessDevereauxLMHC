Jess Devereaux, LMHC

Child & Adolescent Therapy · Rhode Island

I work with children and adolescents in Rhode Island navigating anxiety, mood concerns, school stress, social challenges, and life transitions. They deserve a safe and supportive space to explore their emotions, strengthen coping skills, and grow in confidence.

Therapy is tailored to each child’s age, strengths, and needs. With younger children, sessions may include play and creative activities. With teens, therapy focuses on insight, skill-building, and open discussion. I also partner closely with parents to reinforce progress and support emotional growth at home.

With 13 years of experience as a school counselor, I specialize in anxiety, mood concerns, emotional regulation, and school-related stress. My approach integrates CBT, solution-focused strategies, and mindfulness to build resilience and lasting skills.

Starting therapy can feel overwhelming. My goal is to create a calm, understanding space for children and teens while collaborating with parents to support growth. I would love to connect and see if my approach is a fit for your child and family.
