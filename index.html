About

Warm, mindful, and evidence-informed counseling—tailored to the client in front of me.

My mission is to create a supportive, authentic space where children, teens, adults, and families feel truly seen and understood. Through mindfulness, creative expression, and evidence-based strategies, I help clients build emotional awareness, develop coping skills, and strengthen their sense of self.

My goal is to empower each client to navigate challenges with confidence, compassion, and resilience—while honoring their pace, strengths, and values.
