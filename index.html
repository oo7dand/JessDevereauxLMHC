About Jessica

Jessica Devereaux, LMHC, is a licensed mental health counselor providing calm, collaborative therapy for adults, teens, and families. Her style is warm, curious, and grounded in evidence-based practices.

Jessica integrates cognitive behavioral therapy, mindfulness, and relational work — always centering your goals and pace. She believes change happens in small, sustainable steps, supported by a safe therapeutic relationship.
