About

My mission is to create a supportive, authentic space where children, teens, adults, and families feel truly seen and understood. Together, we’ll build practical tools for emotional regulation, communication, and resilience—at a pace that feels safe and manageable.

Availability: Telehealth available. In-person options may be limited depending on schedule.

Services

Child & Adolescent Therapy

Helping kids and teens build emotional awareness, coping skills, confidence, and healthy routines.

Family Support

Strengthening connection, communication, and boundaries—especially during times of transition or stress.

Adult Therapy

Support for anxiety, life transitions, burnout, self-esteem, and creating more sustainable patterns.

Mind–Body Integration

Grounding skills and practical strategies to help you feel more steady, connected, and resilient.
