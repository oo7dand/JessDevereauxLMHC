About Jessica

Hi, I’m Jess Devereaux, LMHC, a licensed mental health counselor in Rhode Island and certified school counselor in Massachusetts. After 13 years working as a school counselor, I’ve transitioned into private practice, where I continue my passion for supporting children, adolescents, and their families.

In my years in schools—five at the preschool level and seven in middle school—I had the privilege of helping students navigate academic pressures, social challenges, emotional struggles, and moments of crisis. I led counseling groups, supported social-emotional learning, and collaborated closely with families, teachers, and IEP teams to ensure each student felt supported in every aspect of their growth.

My counseling style is warm, relational, and authentic. I believe the therapeutic relationship is the foundation for change, and I strive to create a space where kids and teens feel seen, heard, and understood. My work is trauma-informed and rooted in mindfulness, CBT, and DBT principles. I often help clients explore how emotions show up in the body, and I’m gradually weaving in journaling and art therapy techniques to help with expression and self-discovery.

I offer both in-person and telehealth sessions, providing flexible options for families. While my practice currently focuses on children and adolescents, I look forward to expanding to serve clients across the lifespan as my practice grows.

My goal is to help every client build the tools to understand themselves more deeply, manage life’s ups and downs, and move forward with greater confidence and calm.

She integrates cognitive behavioral therapy, mindfulness, and relational work — always centering your goals, values, and pace. Jessica believes that meaningful change happens in small, sustainable steps, supported by a strong therapeutic relationship.
