Child & Adolescent Therapy · Rhode Island

I work with children and adolescents in Rhode Island navigating anxiety, mood concerns, school stress, social challenges, and life transitions. They deserve a safe and supportive space to explore their emotions, strengthen coping skills, and grow in confidence.

Therapy is tailored to each child’s age, strengths, and needs. With younger children, sessions may include play and creative activities. With teens, therapy focuses on insight, skill-building, and open discussion. I also partner with parents to reinforce progress and support emotional growth at home.
